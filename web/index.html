sections & hints

    
A hint marks where to try something, and for how
      long: the warm-up performs it and the reward pays a little for it.
      Scope by checkpoint section, a place you drove to, speed, or any mix.
      Forced gas/brake/steer apply to the warm-up only.

checkpoint sizes

    
The game exposes no dimensions for a checkpoint, so the
      global half-width and height above are the default and these are the
      exceptions, by gate index. A checkpoint that never counts is usually a
      sizing problem.

    

    


    
Surfaces

    

      Per-wheel, per-step reward for whatever each tyre is touching; negative
      is a penalty. Prefer End episode over a big penalty for "stop
      crashing into the barriers". Needs surfaces switched on above.
    

    

  


  

    
Sections

    

      
      
    

    
Per-section times, from every episode that got that far.
      gap is median minus best — how much is sitting on the
      table in that piece of track. The biggest gap is where to work. A low
      reached is not a slow section, it is where the car keeps
      dying, which is a different problem.

    

  


  

    
Why it's changing

    

    
Reward breakdown per episode plus SAC's own optimiser
      state. Read the dominant term first: it is what the policy is actually
      being shaped by, whatever you intended.

  


  

    
Checkpoints & snapshots

    

      
      
      
    

    

    
Restoring copies a snapshot over the live model; the
      replay buffer is left alone, since off-policy experience stays valid for
      whichever policy you roll back to. Stop the trainer first, then Start
      with resume ticked.

  


  

    
Saved runs

    

    
Real .Replay.Gbx files, lifted out of the
      game's own autosave folder on every personal best before the next one
      overwrites it. Traces cover the runs that got further without finishing.
